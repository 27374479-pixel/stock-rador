## Why 三美股份

The selection is not based on “refrigerants are hot.”

By the cutoff:
- HFC quota control was an official multi-year supply regime.
- 三美's H1 refrigerant revenue was about RMB2.42bn, roughly 86% of H1 group revenue.
- H1 attributable net profit was about RMB0.995bn (+159% YoY).
- H1 operating cash flow was about RMB0.944bn.
- Company-reported refrigerant realized ASP was about RMB39,038/t (+56.5%).
- Early-September market quotes for key HFC grades were materially above the H1 realized basket.

三美 therefore had a cleaner **price -> realized ASP -> margin -> profit -> cash** bridge than a generic quota beneficiary.

The narrative was already crowded and the stock had already rerated strongly. The selection survives only because the point-in-time valuation was materially lower than the diversified peer and did not require further September spot-price appreciation merely to support the base case.

## Catalyst freshness

The quota thesis itself is old information. The potentially incremental information is the **new post-H1 step-up in quoted HFC prices after company results had already proven the transmission mechanism**.

This is treated conservatively: the lock does not assume spot R32 equals the company's realized product basket, and it does not annualize the September quote.

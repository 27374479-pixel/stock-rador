# Skill V3.3 December 2025 — 20d checkpoint decision

**Decision: EXIT at the first checkpoint.**

This decision is frozen **before any 江西铜业 price after 2026-01-13 is opened**.

## Checkpoint state

The physical copper regime was still strong. Copper had moved above USD13,000/t around the checkpoint and supply concerns had not disappeared.

But the stock-specific **incremental surprise** was no longer as attractive:

- copper itself had entered a record-price regime;
- the shortage + AI/grid-demand narrative had become broadly recognized;
- Chinese industrial buyers were already showing demand-destruction behavior at record prices;
- no new company financial report was available by the checkpoint to prove a new earnings step-up beyond the Q1-Q3 2025 base.

The V3.3 decomposition therefore changed from:

`tight physical regime -> plausible company earnings upside -> still-open expectation gap`

to:

`tight physical regime -> likely positive company economics -> shrinking incremental surprise / expectation gap`

## Price context already legally opened

Only the frozen 20-trading-day segment was opened:

- entry: 2025-12-15

- 江西铜业 net return: +42.1%

The **EXIT** is not justified by “we already made enough.” It would still be the checkpoint decision if the stock return were weaker, because the causal re-underwriting shows expectation saturation and emerging demand destruction.

## AI-server power-density step-up — signal 2023-06-14

TrendForce's May/June work showed a rapid AI-server shipment expansion and estimated that AI-server power-supply demand was roughly two to three times a conventional server. The candidate mapping was then restricted to companies with pre-signal evidence of existing server/data-center power products and delivery capability.

欧陆通 ranks first because its 2022 report already showed server-power revenue above RMB596m, more than doubling year on year, while server-power gross profit rose roughly 160% and higher-end PSU mix was improving.

麦格米特 also passes because its network/server-power business already served international server leaders and had entered a leading domestic server vendor before the AI-server demand shock.

中恒电气 and 科士达 pass more narrowly. Their products are primarily data-center facility power / HVDC / UPS rather than the server PSU itself, so the economic bridge is less direct and their monetization scores are lower.

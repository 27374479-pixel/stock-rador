## Why the obvious AI themes were rejected

The December memory and laser bottlenecks were real. TrendForce reported enterprise SSD buyers scrambling for components and >25% QoQ contract-price pressure, while 800G+ optical demand was creating an EML/CW laser bottleneck.

But the A-share price bridge failed:
- 香农芯创: ~120x simple annualized 9M earnings after >200% prior-60d return.
- 北京君正: ~135x.
- 源杰科技: ~411x after >400% prior-180d return.
- 仕佳光子: ~108x after >400% prior-180d return.

V3.3 therefore records **NO TRADE** rather than rewarding a correct industry story.

## Why 江西铜业 survives

The copper story was also mature: copper was near record highs and the stock had already risen ~98% over 180 trading days.

The difference is that:
- the company owned five operating copper mines;
- Q1-Q3 attributable profit was already about RMB6.02bn;
- operating cash flow was about RMB6.29bn;
- simple annualized Q1-Q3 earnings implied about 18x P/E, not a triple-digit multiple.

The lock does **not** assume copper rises further. It requires the tight balance to continue converting into company-realized economics.

## V3.3 checkpoint contract

Two checkpoints were frozen before outcomes:
- **20 trading days**: test catalyst freshness, physical tightness, company bridge and expectation-gap persistence.
- **60 trading days**: test whether the physical copper regime is still creating incremental earnings surprise.

Only the path through the first checkpoint may be opened now. The 20-day checkpoint decision must be committed before any later selected-stock price segment is fetched.

## U.S. transformer bottleneck

The real-world bottleneck was strong, but the Skill decision was **NO TRADE**.

伊戈尔 remained Watch even though its pre-signal stock returns were weak (20d -14.1%, 60d -15.3%, 180d -10.2%). The problem was not price. The missing link was point-in-time proof that the company's products/customers matched the specific U.S. utility distribution-transformer shortage rather than adjacent PV/industrial transformer demand.

This is an important Skill-first behavior: a cheap or weak stock cannot repair an incomplete causal/product mapping.

Its future price was deliberately not opened.

## What the window teaches

The window is encouraging for the Skill-first architecture because the strongest outcome came from a general commodity/supply-chain signal rather than a pre-programmed AI/new-energy theme.

It also adds two useful distinctions:

1. **Large prior appreciation is not automatically "priced in."** 洛阳钼业 had already risen almost 48% over 60 trading days and still generated another +31.1% 20-day excess after the signal. What matters is whether new information changes the magnitude/duration of the earnings regime.
2. **Shock half-life matters.** The concentrate squeeze produced a strong near-term equity response but only modest 250-day excess. A supply squeeze/commodity shock should not automatically be treated like a multi-year architecture transition.

Neither observation becomes a hard rule from one window. They enter the question bank as evidence for experimental questions and will be tested again.

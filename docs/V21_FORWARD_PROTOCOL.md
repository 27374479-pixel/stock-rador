# V2.1 forward-only revision

V2.1 is effective starting with the untouched 2022-06 window. It is **not** retroactively applied to the consumed March 2022 window.

March showed that two equally real shortage narratives can have very different investment implications. A PLC shortage paired with evidence that domestic suppliers could actually deliver and take share produced strong long-horizon excess in that consumed sample, whereas a broad MCU shortage did not. That observation is used only to formulate a forward rule.

## New requirement: monetization/capture

A shortage now needs a causal profit bridge. The candidate is scored on:

- ability to deliver while competitors cannot;
- contemporaneous evidence of substitution or share gain;
- ability to retain higher price/margin rather than merely suffer higher input cost;
- whether the benefit can survive normalization;
- exposure to the same upstream constraint.

A company selling the scarce product does **not** automatically benefit.

## New penalty: reversal risk

Before selecting a cyclical shortage theme, V2.1 explicitly searches for contemporaneous contrary signals:

- spot prices already rolling over;
- inventories building;
- demand destruction or order cancellation.

This is separate from the stock's own prior return. It prevents a physically tight market from being treated as a fresh positive signal after the economic impulse has already started reversing.

The candidate threshold is raised from 15 to 20 and monetization score must be at least 5. These settings are frozen before the 2022-06 outcome data is viewed.

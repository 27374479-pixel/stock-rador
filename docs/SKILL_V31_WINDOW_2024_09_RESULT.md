## Learning classification

This was not primarily a source-verification or valuation failure.

The operating signal was real, the company was directly exposed, and the stock did not require an extreme earnings assumption. Yet the excess return decayed quickly after the initial repricing.

This independently repeats the 2024-03 copper-concentrate pattern:
- 2024-03 洛阳钼业: +31.1% 20d excess -> +5.7% 250d;
- 2024-09 中国铝业: +15.9% 20d excess -> -21.3% 250d.

Two independent commodity/bottleneck windows now support promoting **causal half-life** from an Experimental question into a forward-only core research requirement.

The lesson is not “sell all commodity stocks after 20 days.” The generalizable lesson is:

> Before calling a stock a Candidate, state how long the economic edge should persist, what observable variables would show that it is normalizing, and why the intended research horizon matches that causal duration.

Valuation and basis reconciliation remain necessary, but they cannot manufacture a multi-year edge from a short-lived repricing shock.

## Failure decomposition

The real-world signal itself was real. Transpacific spot rates had surged and the company had genuine transpacific exposure plus demonstrated revenue-per-TEU sensitivity.

The main failure is better classified as **expectation / timing / marginal-information failure**:

1. The tariff-pause frontload mechanism was already being discussed weeks before the frozen signal.
2. By the cutoff, spot rates had already moved dramatically and the stock had risen roughly 15.9% over 60 trading days and 48.7% over 180 days.
3. The event was explicitly temporary. A low trailing/cutoff earnings multiple was not sufficient proof that the *new June information delta* was underpriced.
4. A company can have a clean earnings bridge and a low normalized multiple while still offering little incremental alpha if the market has already anticipated the temporary catalyst.

The valuation bridge prevented an obviously expensive selection, but it did not measure **catalyst freshness** well enough.

## Forward-only learning

2025-06 is not rescored.

A new Experimental question is added for future event-repricing cases:

> What part of the current signal is genuinely new relative to what the market already anticipated, and can that new information reach company earnings before the event expires?

This is intentionally **not** promoted into a hard threshold from one miss. V3.2 remains unchanged for the untouched 2025-09 window.

The failure is useful because it prevents the Skill from learning the wrong lesson that low P/E plus a dramatic short-term industry indicator is sufficient for an event trade.

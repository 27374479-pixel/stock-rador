# V2.2 forward-only protocol

V2.2 becomes effective with the untouched **2023-09** window. All windows through 2023-06 are consumed and will never be presented as fresh validation for this revision.

## Why V2.2 exists

Multiple consumed windows now show the same failure mode from different directions.

A correct industry narrative is not sufficient. V2.1 could still give too much credit to a company because a broad business segment was related to the theme, even when the **theme-specific product** had not yet been shown to be economically important.

Examples from consumed windows include:

- liquid-cooling secondaries where the technology direction was right but durable company-level capture differed sharply;
- the June 2023 AI-server power window, where the power-density step-up was real but broad server/data-center power exposure did not translate into durable stock alpha;
- the December 2022 antipyretic case, where direct exposure existed but the shock was short-lived and heavily priced.

Successful consumed cases more often had pre-signal evidence of actual shipment, substitution, customer deployment or a product transition that could move company economics.

## New factor: incremental earnings bridge

V2.2 adds five point-in-time fields:

- **bottleneckSpecificRevenueMateriality (0-3):** how meaningful the exact affected product is to company revenue/profit, not the parent segment;
- **shipmentOrOrderProof (0-3):** existing batch shipment, order, qualification or customer deployment evidence;
- **unitEconomicsLeverage (0-2):** credible higher ASP, margin, content-per-system or share gain;
- **durationVisibility (0-2):** whether the demand change is likely to persist long enough to affect estimates;
- **adjacencyPenalty (0-3):** penalty when the mapping relies on a related but economically different product layer.

A candidate must score at least 5 on this bridge and must have at least a 2 in either bottleneck-specific materiality or shipment/order proof.

The total Candidate threshold rises from 20 to 25 because the new factor adds a maximum of 10 points.

## What V2.2 is not

It is not an anti-momentum rule. The March 2023 optical window showed that very strong prior price appreciation can coexist with a still-underestimated architecture transition.

It is also not a preference for AI or optics. V2.2 is sector-agnostic. The new requirement is simply that the real-world shock has a visible path into **incremental company earnings**, not merely thematic adjacency.

No consumed window will be rerun to claim improved historical performance. The first honest test of V2.2 starts with 2023-09.

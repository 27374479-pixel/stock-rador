# Research Question Bank

Questions are intentionally more important than fixed weights. The same core questions apply across industries; specialized modules activate only after the shock type is identified.

## Core questions — always ask

### Reality
- What changed in the real world?
- What is the strongest primary or professional evidence?
- Is there an independent second root?
- What is the strongest contradictory evidence?

### Causality
- Why did this happen now?
- What bottleneck or constraint transmits the change into economics?
- Who gains and who loses?
- Does the profit pool expand or merely shift?

### Company capture
- Does the company sell the exact product/service that captures the change?
- Is the relevant business material to revenue or profit?
- Is it already qualified/shipping at the cutoff?
- Can it deliver more without being constrained by the same bottleneck?

### Earnings bridge
- How does the change alter volume, price, mix or market share?
- How does that reach gross profit, EBIT, EPS and cash flow?
- Which link is weakest?
- What evidence would turn the bridge from plausible into proven?

### Expectations / price
- What did the market already know?
- What had consensus already revised?
- Is the stock move proportional to the likely earnings change?
- Is the narrative saturated?
- What assumption must be wrong for the stock still to be cheap after a large rally?

### Falsification
- What are the three strongest ways this thesis fails?
- What new evidence would cause an immediate downgrade to Watch/No Trade?
- Is there a contrary signal already visible?

## Shock-type router

### Structural architecture transition
Examples: 400G -> 800G, air cooling -> liquid cooling, legacy controller -> new architecture.

Ask:
- Does the new architecture increase unit value per system?
- Is there mandatory content, or optional adjacency?
- What is the adoption S-curve?
- Does the candidate already mass-produce the required component?
- Can competitors qualify quickly?
- Does the transition create a multi-year installed-base change?

### Supply shortage / bottleneck
Ask:
- Can the candidate actually ship while competitors cannot?
- Is the candidate constrained by the same input?
- How fast can new capacity enter?
- Is scarcity creating higher margin, or merely higher costs?
- Is the shortage regional or global?

### Demand acceleration
Ask:
- Is demand pull-forward or net-new demand?
- Is it repeatable?
- Is customer capex/order evidence visible?
- Can the company serve incremental demand now?
- Does demand convert to recurring revenue or a one-time sale?

### Geographic market opening
Ask:
- Does the company already have certification/channel/service in the target geography?
- Is export capacity available?
- Are tariffs, logistics or local standards barriers?
- Is local competition weak enough for share gain?

### Share substitution
Ask:
- Why is the incumbent losing share?
- Is the substitution due to price, availability, technology or policy?
- Is the new supplier already qualified?
- Is the share gain reversible when the incumbent recovers?

### Cyclical inventory / price reversal
Ask:
- Where is inventory held across the chain?
- At what approximate acquisition cost?
- Who benefits from rising prices and who suffers from replacement cost?
- Are receivables/debt/working capital large enough to overwhelm gross-margin benefit?
- Is the price turn caused by demand recovery or supply cuts?
- Does downstream demand validate the cycle turn?

### Cost-curve shock
Ask:
- Is the company a price taker or price setter?
- Can input-cost changes be passed through?
- What is the lag between input price and selling price?
- Is there high-cost or low-cost inventory?
- Does lower input cost invite price competition?

### Policy / regulatory change
Ask:
- Is the policy funded and executable?
- Who is legally eligible?
- Is there procurement/reimbursement/tender evidence?
- What is the implementation timeline?
- Could local rules or administrative delays break the thesis?

### Temporary event / one-off order
Ask:
- Is the order repeatable?
- Is capacity reusable after the event?
- Does the event change the installed base or only one period's revenue?
- Is the stock being valued as if a temporary spike were permanent?

## Experimental questions from consumed backtests

These questions may improve analysis immediately but are not hard gates until promoted.

### X1 — Inventory/working-capital capture in cyclical rebounds
Origin: 2023-12 memory-price rebound.
- Did the company buy inventory before or after the price turn?
- Is inventory a source of margin expansion or write-down/financing risk?
- How much cash is required to sustain the inventory position?
- Does debt/interest expense dilute the apparent gross-margin benefit?

Status: **experimental**. One consumed window so far.

### X2 — Duration-adjusted momentum
Origin: 2022-12 antipyretic miss versus 2023-03 800G success.
- Is the stock's prior rally larger than the duration-weighted earnings opportunity?
- Is the shock measured in weeks, quarters or years?
- Does a large prior move reflect temporary hype or rational repricing of a structural transition?

Status: **experimental**. Supported by multiple contrasting consumed windows; candidate for promotion in a future protocol version after formal review.

### X3 — Primary product versus adjacency inside a correct theme
Origin: 2023-03 liquid-cooling dispersion.
- Is the product a mandatory value-bearing component or merely adjacent?
- Is shipment evidence project-specific or repeatable?
- What percentage of future profit can the theme realistically move?

Status: **experimental**, with supporting evidence from more than one cluster but not yet encoded as a hard gate beyond the existing earnings-bridge requirement.
